# Product

## Register

product

## Users

CareerPilot CN serves Chinese campus-recruitment candidates applying to central and state-owned enterprises, especially candidates who must compare several roles from one employer while keeping sensitive personal materials local. They use the product as an evidence desk: confirming Facts, comparing qualification rules, choosing a role, reviewing a tailored resume, and preparing an application without surrendering the final submission decision.

## Product Purpose

Turn a fragmented job-search process into one trustworthy, deterministic workflow:

`resume evidence -> multi-role Campaign -> rule confirmation -> explainable ranking -> human role selection -> Fact Diff tailoring -> preview confirmation -> manifest-bound DOCX/PDF -> application package -> stop before submit`.

Success means every published claim remains traceable to confirmed Evidence, every material resume change is visible and approved, style choices alter presentation rather than facts, and final artifacts remain reproducible and auditable.

## Brand Personality

Steady, clear, trustworthy. The product should feel like a careful application desk operated with the candidate, not a growth-hacking assistant acting on the candidate's behalf. Its voice is direct Simplified Chinese, calm under uncertainty, and explicit about blocks, missing evidence, and decisions that still belong to the user.

## Anti-references

- Generic overseas ATS dashboards that reduce a decision to an unexplained percentage.
- Flashy startup landing pages, neon AI gradients, glassmorphism, and decorative automation theater.
- “One-click mass apply” growth hacks or any interface that treats submission volume as success.
- Resume-template galleries that show attractive mockups but hide pagination, text-layer, Fact traceability, or export differences.
- Social-media resume examples treated as candidate facts, certified recruiter truth, or verbatim templates.

## Design Principles

- **Evidence stays visible.** Important recommendations, rewrites, and blocks expose their source Facts, rules, hashes, or QA state.
- **Preview the real artifact.** Style selection uses renders from the same normalized model as DOCX/PDF, not decorative thumbnails.
- **Calm density over decoration.** Information can be compact, but hierarchy, alignment, and readable Chinese typography must remain clear.
- **Human confirmation is a product feature.** Selecting a role, accepting a rewrite, authorizing a photo, and recording external submission are deliberate steps.
- **Private by default.** Candidate data and third-party reference images live in the ignored user layer; anonymous fixtures are used for tests and shared previews.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for the Web UI. All critical workflows must be keyboard reachable, retain visible focus, work without color-only meaning, support reduced motion, and keep Chinese body text legible at common Windows display scaling. Resume recommendations must not infer protected or sensitive attributes; photo and political-status disclosure remain explicit, revocable per artifact, and never globally assumed.
